Canonical Baseline & Benchmark Datasets
---------------------------------------

- `MNIST Handwritten Datits <http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/>`_ — Classic 70,000-image grayscale baseline for computer vision and classification sanity checks.
- `CIFAR-10 & CIFAR-100 <https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html>`_ — Foundational multi-class small-image benchmarks for rapid deep learning prototyping.
- `ImageNet <https://www.image-net.org/>`_ — Large-scale hierarchical visual database organized according to the WordNet hierarchy.
- `COCO (Common Objects in Context) <https://cocodataset.org/>`_ — Standard benchmark for object detection, instance segmentation, and image captioning.
- `Open Images Dataset V7 and Extensions <https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/index.html>`_ — Massive dataset of images annotated with object labels, segmentations, and visual relationships.
- `Project Gutenberg <https://www.gutenberg.org/>`_ — Over 70,000 free public-domain literary texts, widely used as a clean baseline corpus for NLP.

Astrophysics, Space Telescopes & Cosmology

- `NASA Exoplanet Archive <https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/>`_ — Confirmed exoplanet catalogs, transit light curves, and stellar parameters.

- `VizieR & CDS Astronomical Catalogs <https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/>`_ — World center for astronomical observation catalogs, astrometry, and photometry (including Gaia data).

- `NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) <https://pds.nasa.gov/>`_ — Digital data products, imagery, and telemetry from NASA planetary space missions.


Biomedical Literature, Genetics, Microbiology & Genomics
--------------------------------------------------------

- `PubMed Annual Baseline & Daily Updates <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/download/>`_ — Complete yearly snapshots and incremental XML feeds of biomedical literature citations and abstracts from MEDLINE.
- `PubMed Central (PMC) Open Access Subset <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/openftlist/>`_ — Bulk machine-readable full-text research articles with Creative Commons licenses optimized for text mining and NLP.
- `NCBI GenBank Sequence Database <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/>`_ — Comprehensive NIH genetic sequence database containing all publicly available DNA and RNA sequences via FTP and Entrez Direct.
- `NCBI Datasets API & CLI <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/>`_ — Unified command-line and programmatic interface for downloading annotated genomes, gene annotations, and viral/microbial assemblies.
- `BV-BRC (Bacterial and Viral Bioinformatics Resource Center) <https://www.bv-brc.org/>`_ — Integrated genomics platform supporting comparative systems biology and omics data for bacterial and viral infectious agents.
- `UniProt <https://www.uniprot.org/>`_ — Universal Protein Resource providing comprehensive, high-quality protein sequence and functional annotation data.
- `European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) <https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/>`_ — Comprehensive provider of nucleotide sequencing data archiving and collaborative sequence releases across Europe.
- `SILVA Ribosomal RNA Database <https://www.arb-silva.de/>`_ — High-quality aligned ribosomal RNA sequence datasets for microbial ecology, taxonomy, and microbiome analysis.
- `KEGG (Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes) <https://www.kegg.jp/>`_ — Database resource for mapping genomic information to higher-order functional networks and metabolic pathways.
- `EcoCyc <https://ecocyc.org/>`_ — Comprehensive model organism database detailing the genome and metabolic pathways of *Escherichia coli*.

Radiology, Medical Imaging & Clinical Subspecialties
----------------------------------------------------

- `The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA) <https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/>`_ — Large-scale open repository of medical DICOM imaging datasets spanning radiology, oncology, and multi-organ pathology.
- `CBIS-DDSM (Curated Breast Imaging Subset of DDSM) <https://wiki.cancerimagingarchive.net/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=22516629>`_ — Standardized benchmark dataset for mammography, breast ultrasound/MRI, calcifications, and mass segmentation.
- `INbreast Mammography Database <https://www.ines-patrocinio.pt/inbreast>`_ — High-resolution digital mammography database annotated by clinical experts with masses and microcalcifications.
- `Stanford MURA (Musculoskeletal Radiographs) <https://stanfordmlgroup.github.io/competitions/mura/>`_ — Large dataset of skeletal and musculoskeletal bone X-rays for abnormality detection.
- `NIH Chest X-ray14 <https://nihcc.app.box.com/v/ChestXray-NIHCC>`_ — Over 100,000 anonymized frontal chest X-ray images with disease labels covering cardiothoracic and pulmonary conditions.
- `RSNA Intracranial Hemorrhage Detection Dataset <https://www.kaggle.com/c/rsna-intracranial-hemorrhage-detection>`_ — Emergency and neuroradiological head CT scans annotated for acute intracranial hemorrhages.
- `RSNA Pediatric Bone Age Machine Learning Challenge <https://www.kaggle.com/c/rsna-bone-age>`_ — Pediatric skeletal radiology dataset of hand radiographs for developmental bone age assessment.
- `BraTS (Brain Tumor Segmentation Benchmark) <https://www.med.upenn.gov/sbia/brats2021/data.html>`_ — Multimodal MRI scans (T1, T2, FLAIR) for neuroradiological brain tumor segmentation.
- `Abdominal CT Organ Segmentation (kits19 / AMOS) <https://amos22.grand-challenge.org/>`_ — Multi-organ abdominal CT imaging datasets annotated for clinical segmentation.
- `Open-i (Open Access Biomedical Image Search) <https://openi.nlm.nih.gov/>`_ — National Library of Medicine open image search engine indexing clinical, radiological, and osteopathic medical figures.
